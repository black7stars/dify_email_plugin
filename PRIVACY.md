# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection and Usage

This QQ Email Crawler plugin is designed to help users access and manage their QQ email accounts. We take user privacy seriously and have implemented the following measures:

### Data Collection
- **Email Credentials**: The plugin requires QQ email account credentials (username/password or access tokens) to authenticate and access email data.
- **Email Content**: The plugin may access email content including sender information, subject lines, message bodies, and attachments for the purpose of email management and processing.
- **Metadata**: Basic email metadata such as timestamps, message IDs, and folder information may be collected.

### Data Usage
- **Local Processing**: All email data processing occurs locally within the user's environment.
- **No External Sharing**: Email data and credentials are not transmitted to external servers or third parties.
- **Temporary Storage**: Email data may be temporarily cached for performance optimization but is not permanently stored.

### Data Security
- **Encryption**: All sensitive data including credentials are handled with appropriate encryption measures.
- **Authentication**: Secure authentication protocols are used to connect to QQ email servers.
- **Access Control**: Users have full control over which email data the plugin can access.

### User Rights
- **Data Access**: Users can view what data the plugin accesses through the Dify interface.
- **Revocation**: Users can revoke the plugin's access to their email data at any time.
- **Deletion**: No persistent storage of user data is maintained by the plugin.

### Compliance
This plugin complies with relevant data protection regulations including GDPR and local privacy laws. Users should ensure their usage complies with QQ email's terms of service.

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the plugin author.
